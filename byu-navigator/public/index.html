              About us

              Why we created BYU Navigator

              As international students, we saw how hard it can be to find everything you need across different websites and offices. Information about applying, housing, classes, campus support, and life beyond campus can be scattered. We created BYU Navigator to bring helpful campus and selected off-campus resources together in one place, so students can find guidance from their first steps toward BYU through graduation, organized around the journey and the support they need along the way.

              This guide helps you find resources. For official requirements and decisions, confirm details with the appropriate BYU office.

              Help us improve

              Tell us what is missing, what was helpful, or what you would like us to add.
